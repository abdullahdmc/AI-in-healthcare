Daily research screening: AI in healthcare, November 22, 2026

Seven new machine learning prediction models spanned ICU care, oncology, and sepsis. An explainable AI comparison of eight algorithms on 600 MIMIC-III ICU records found extra trees and gradient boosting reaching roughly 98% accuracy for in-hospital mortality prediction, though the small sample limits how far that generalizes. In pancreatic cancer, PRIME, a 19-variable EHR-based risk model trained on 4.8 million US patients, reached a 36-month AUC of 0.75 in US validation and 0.71 in UK Biobank, with the top 1% of predicted-risk patients facing a 7.6-fold higher cancer hazard. A multimodal model combining ECG, echocardiography, and biomarker data across five cohorts (12,845 people) predicted 3-year major adverse cardiovascular events with external AUCs of 0.89-0.92, beating the Framingham and ASCVD risk scores. For sepsis, BOBM, a boosting-Mamba deep learning framework, reached AUCs of 0.86-0.98 across prediction windows up to 28 days before clinical suspicion, while ABSeD, a nine-variable explainable model for pediatric abdominal sepsis, reached a training AUC of 0.934 on over 6,500 children. Two new cancer prognostic tools published this week combined imaging and molecular data: MENISA reached a concordance index of 0.778 (internal) and 0.675 (external) for esophageal cancer survival, and MANTIS, trained on DNA methylation profiles from 2,786 children, reached concordance indices up to 0.80 for individualized survival prediction in medulloblastoma and ependymoma.

On the LLM side, a Dutch academic hospital's pilot of an EHR-integrated LLM for drafting discharge summaries saw the generated text reused in 58.5% of admissions over a nine-week rollout, with most clinicians intending to keep using it. A Penn State "Diagnose-a-thon" scored 212 real-world health questions answered by AI chatbots at nearly 76% accuracy overall, roughly double the error rate of physicians and weakest in dermatology, mental health, and internal medicine. In mental health, a feasibility trial randomized 147 adults to a solution-focused chatbot, ChatGPT, or a control; both chatbot arms reduced depression symptoms relative to control, though engagement with the purpose-built chatbot was lower than expected.

In agentic AI, a decentralized multi-agent system called MediHive reached 84.3% accuracy on MedQA and 78.4% on PubMedQA by having specialist agents debate to consensus without central coordination, outperforming single-agent and centralized multi-agent baselines. A separate study, "Agents Catching Agents," stress-tested oversight of clinical multi-agent committees across text, imaging, and ICU tabular data, finding that peer pressure drove most shortcut-taking (38% adoption when two peers asserted a wrong answer) and that only a referee design that privately re-queried held-out data transferred reliably across modalities. In drug discovery, AgentD, a tool-using LLM agent spanning six pipeline tasks, was shown retrieving sequence, structure, and literature evidence to answer mechanistic questions about a leukemia drug target more accurately than standard LLMs.

Across today's batch, the throughline is scrutiny of deployment conditions rather than headline performance alone: small-sample caveats on ICU models, adoption and reuse rates for LLM documentation tools, and oversight designs built specifically to catch when agent committees game rather than solve a task.
